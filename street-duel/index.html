Street Duel: Football & Soccer

Street Duel is a fast 1v1 street soccer game that works offline. First to 5 goals, or the leader after 3 minutes, wins. Ties go to a golden goal.

  • Quick offline 1v1 matches
  • Street League with 15 rivals on three courts
  • Street King endless mode
  • Floating joystick, swipe shots and a skill button
  • Six power-ups
  • Kits, boots and ball skins
  • Player training
  • No purchases required

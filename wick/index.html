Wick: One Light Left

The Shade swallowed every light in the city but one, and Wick carries it. Fight endless waves of shadow creatures with your scythe, pick an upgrade after every wave, beat giant bosses and bring the city's lights back. Free to play in your browser.

  • Fast pixel-art action with a scythe, dashes and a three-hit combo
  • Waves of shades with their own tricks: diving moths, shooting wisps, shielded wardens
  • Six story bosses, cutscenes and two endings
  • Upgrades every wave, unlockable weapons and skins
  • Checkpoints at waves 11, 21 and 31
  • Keyboard and mouse, or touch controls on phones
  • Free, plays in the browser with no download

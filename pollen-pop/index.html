Pollen Pop: Bee Sort Puzzle

Pollen Pop is a calm sort puzzle. Tap a hive to send its bees out; each bee collects one pollen tile of its colour from the hex board. Clear the board to reveal the picture and add it to your Album.

  • 300 levels and 60 pictures to collect
  • Fair levels: every level can be solved without boosters
  • Daily gift, three daily missions and a daily challenge
  • Undo, Shuffle and Extra Station boosters
  • No timers, no stamina
  • Colour-blind mode
  • Plays offline, no in-app purchases

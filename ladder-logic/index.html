Ladder Logic: No Luck Needed

The board game you know, without the luck. Every level gives you a set of dice you can see, and you choose the order. Land exactly on the star to win, and use the ladders and snakes as tools on the way.

  • 160 levels in 7 worlds
  • Something new in every world: ice, sleepy snakes, reversed dice, portals, hungry snakes, keys and locked ladders
  • A new daily challenge and a streak to keep
  • Solve a level in the fewest moves to earn its crown
  • You can never lose a level: undo and try again
  • Hints when you need them
  • Pixel art and a calm pace
  • Sign-in is optional: a free Google account keeps your progress on a new phone
  • English and Arabic

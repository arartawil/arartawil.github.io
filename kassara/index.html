Kassara: Crush & Collect

Take the wheel of a little bulldozer and smash rocks across four mines. Every rock cracks and bursts into gems that fly onto the pile on your back. Sell them at the market, then upgrade your blade, bag and engine. Each mine, from Wadi Rum to the Volcano and the Gold Cave, ends with a giant boss rock.

  • Satisfying crushing: rocks shake and gems pour onto your dozer
  • Upgrade your blade, bag and engine
  • Open gates to new zones: sandstone, iron, crystal
  • Hire workers who mine and sell for you, even while you are away
  • Build the crusher to turn gems into gold bars
  • Four mines with a boss rock at the end of each
  • New dozers and perks that stay forever
  • Daily chest, offline earnings, works without internet
  • English and Arabic, one-finger controls

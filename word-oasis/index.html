Word Oasis: Arabic Word Puzzle

Word Oasis is an Arabic word puzzle. Swipe across the letters to form words and fill the grid, travelling through desert worlds level by level.

  • 300 levels across 15 desert worlds
  • Words from three to seven letters
  • Level map and daily challenge
  • Bonus words, hints and letter shuffle
  • Three stars for hint-free wins
  • Full right-to-left Arabic interface
  • Offline, with auto-save

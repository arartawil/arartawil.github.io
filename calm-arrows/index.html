Calm Arrows: Arrow Puzzle

Every level in Calm Arrows is a picture drawn entirely out of arrows: a cat, a heart, a fish, a little house. Tap an arrow and it slides out the way it points. If another arrow is in its way, it bounces back and you lose a water drop. Find the right order, clear every arrow, and the picture fills with color and goes into your Album. No timer, no rush.

  • A calm arrow puzzle with soft sounds, gentle animations and an optional piano loop
  • 3 drops per level, and Try again keeps the arrows you already cleared
  • Long-press any arrow to see its path
  • One-tap hints when you are stuck
  • Collect 120 pictures in your Album
  • A new Daily Puzzle every day, with a streak
  • Themes: Paper, Night, Mint and High contrast
  • Endless levels after the first 20 pictures
  • Plays offline, and ads never interrupt a level

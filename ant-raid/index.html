Ant Raid

Ant Raid is a one-thumb strategy game. Hatch ant squads, draw a pheromone trail from your tunnels to the enemy hill and watch them swarm.

  • Four ant types
  • Spiders, beetles, roaches and wasps
  • Boss fights
  • 21 hand-made raids in three areas
  • Colony upgrades
  • One-thumb, English and Arabic, offline

Arar Studio: offline games made in Jordan

Arar Studio is an independent game studio from Jordan, run by Arar Al Tawil. We make light, free games that work without internet.

  • Domino Café: Classic Dominoes: Domino Café is a classic dominoes game that works fully offline. Play Draw, Block or All Fives against smart bots or team up with a partner, and race to 101, 151 or 201 points.
  • Word Oasis: Arabic Word Puzzle: Word Oasis is an Arabic word puzzle. Swipe across the letters to form words and fill the grid, travelling through desert worlds level by level.
  • Potion Pour: Color Sort Puzzle: Potion Pour is a relaxing color-sort puzzle. Pour potions between flasks on the alchemist's shelf until each flask holds one color, with no timer and no pressure.
  • Street Duel: Football & Soccer: Street Duel is a fast 1v1 street soccer game that works offline. First to 5 goals, or the leader after 3 minutes, wins. Ties go to a golden goal.
  • Ant Raid: Ant Raid is a one-thumb strategy game. Hatch ant squads, draw a pheromone trail from your tunnels to the enemy hill and watch them swarm.
  • Moaddali: University GPA Calculator: Moaddali is a simple university GPA calculator. Enter your grades and credit hours to get your semester and cumulative GPA, and see what you need to reach your target.
  • Pollen Pop: Bee Sort Puzzle: Pollen Pop is a calm sort puzzle. Tap a hive to send its bees out; each bee collects one pollen tile of its colour from the hex board. Clear the board to reveal the picture and add it to your Album.

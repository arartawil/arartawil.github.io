Arar Studio: offline games made in Jordan

Arar Studio is an independent game studio from Jordan, run by Arar Al Tawil. We make light, free games that work without internet.

  • Domino Café: Classic Dominoes: Domino Café is a classic dominoes game that works fully offline. Play Draw, Block or All Fives against smart bots or team up with a partner, and race to 101, 151 or 201 points.
  • Word Oasis: Arabic Word Puzzle: Word Oasis is an Arabic word puzzle. Swipe across the letters to form words and fill the grid, travelling through desert worlds level by level.
  • Potion Pour: Color Sort Puzzle: Potion Pour is a relaxing color-sort puzzle. Pour potions between flasks on the alchemist's shelf until each flask holds one color, with no timer and no pressure.
  • Street Duel: Football & Soccer: Street Duel is a fast 1v1 street soccer game that works offline. First to 5 goals, or the leader after 3 minutes, wins. Ties go to a golden goal.
  • Ant Raid: Ant Raid is a one-thumb strategy game. Hatch ant squads, draw a pheromone trail from your tunnels to the enemy hill and watch them swarm.
  • Moaddali: University GPA Calculator: Moaddali is a simple university GPA calculator. Enter your grades and credit hours to get your semester and cumulative GPA, and see what you need to reach your target.
  • Pollen Pop: Bee Sort Puzzle: Pollen Pop is a calm sort puzzle. Tap a hive to send its bees out; each bee collects one pollen tile of its colour from the hex board. Clear the board to reveal the picture and add it to your Album.
  • Hand Café: Offline Rummy Game: Hand Café is Jordanian Hand, the classic rummy card game, and it works fully offline. Play against the computer on your own or in Partner Hand, two against two. Lay down your melds, get rid of your cards first, and the lowest total wins.
  • Calm Arrows: Arrow Puzzle: Every level in Calm Arrows is a picture drawn entirely out of arrows: a cat, a heart, a fish, a little house. Tap an arrow and it slides out the way it points. If another arrow is in its way, it bounces back and you lose a water drop. Find the right order, clear every arrow, and the picture fills with color and goes into your Album. No timer, no rush.
  • Shifra: Arabic Proverb Puzzle: Shifra is a calm, clever Arabic cryptogram. The phrase at the top is written in numbers, and every number hides a letter. Solve the small picture clues below; each right letter fills every box with the same number, until the whole proverb is revealed.
  • Kassara: Crush & Collect: Take the wheel of a little bulldozer and smash rocks across four mines. Every rock cracks and bursts into gems that fly onto the pile on your back. Sell them at the market, then upgrade your blade, bag and engine. Each mine, from Wadi Rum to the Volcano and the Gold Cave, ends with a giant boss rock.
  • Ladder Logic: No Luck Needed: The board game you know, without the luck. Every level gives you a set of dice you can see, and you choose the order. Land exactly on the star to win, and use the ladders and snakes as tools on the way.
  • Factory Rush: Idle Factory: Start with a pickaxe and build a robot empire. Factory Rush is a relaxing arcade idle game: mine ore, smelt it into iron bars, craft gears and assemble robots. Sell everything for cash, then reinvest to grow your factory.
